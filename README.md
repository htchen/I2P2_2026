# Introduction to Programming II in C and C++

Course-material scaffold for a 16-week semester with 13 lecture notes.

## Course Information

- **Course:** Introduction to Programming II
- **Languages:** C and C++
- **Duration:** 16 weeks
- **Lecture notes:** 13
- **Tuesday meeting:** Evening lecture or scheduled course event
- **Thursday meeting:** Lab or midterm exam
- **First meeting:** September 8, 2026
- **Final exam:** December 22, 2026

---

## Schedule Rules

- Regular lectures take place on Tuesday evenings.
- Labs take place on Thursdays.
- Two Thursday lab meetings will be replaced by midterm exams; dates are TBD.
- The midterm project demo will replace one Tuesday lecture; its date is TBD.
- The final exam takes place on Tuesday, December 22, during Week 16.
- The final project demo will be scheduled on a date after the final exam.
